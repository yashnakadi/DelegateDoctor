Decision

The point
---------
Operator-count delegation barely moved: 96.8% -> 97.4%, a gain of 0.6
percentage points. A tool that reported only operator counts would have called
this model already optimised and moved on. Median latency fell by roughly two
thirds, because a single portable softmax owned about 63% of the runtime.

Not all fallbacks are equal. Optimise runtime, not operator counts.
